Why Open Innovation Matters for "Touch Grass"

When building technology to reconnect humans with the physical biosphere, closed-source cloud AI creates a fundamental paradox: it demands constant cellular connectivity, subjects your movements and foraging locations to corporate telemetry, costs recurring token fees, and relies on servers burning megawatts in faraway data centers.

🏔️ Zero Signal on the Trail

The best trails, old-growth forests, and mountain ridges have zero cellular reception. Grounded runs Gemma 3 entirely on local laptop silicon (RTX 3060 CUDA). You can be miles deep in a national park on airplane mode, and your bioacoustic identifier and garden planner never drop offline.

🔒 Radical Foraging & Trail Privacy

Naturalists, foragers, and runners hold their sacred spots close. When you log a secret chanterelle patch, an endangered owl nest, or your private backyard homestead, that data stays in an encrypted local SQLite database. Not a single byte is uploaded to an ad-targeting server.

⌚ Screen as the Shortest Part

Commercial apps are engineered for engagement and dwell time: feeds, infinite scroll, push notifications. Grounded is engineered for dispatch: 30 seconds to generate your mission or export your watch GPX, then close the laptop. Open-weights models let us tune prompts purely for brevity and outdoor immersion.

💵 Zero Ongoing Cost & Swappable Weights

No API keys, credit cards, or rate limits. Grounded can run Gemma 3:4b, swap to Llama 3.2, or load a fine-tuned ornithology LoRA with one flag in Ollama. Open AI makes nature intelligence accessible to students, park rangers, and homesteaders worldwide for $0 forever.
